# Privacy Policy — AI Trading Toolkit

**Developer:** FX Signal Lab  
**Last Updated:** September 2026

## 1. Overview
AI Trading Toolkit ("the App"), developed by FX Signal Lab, is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

## 2. Information Collection & Storage
- **Trading Journal Data:** All trading journal entries, risk calculations, notes, and user-configured price alerts are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database. We do not transmit or store your personal trading logs on external servers.
- **Market Data Requests:** The App fetches public market data (such as cryptocurrency prices and candlestick charts) directly from public API endpoints. These requests do not transmit personally identifiable information.
- **Third-Party Advertising:** The App may display banner or interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use advertising identifiers and device diagnostic information in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Permissions Requested
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to fetch market data and candlestick price history.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to verify internet availability.
- `android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Used exclusively on Android 13+ to deliver user-configured price threshold alerts.
